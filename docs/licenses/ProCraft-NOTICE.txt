Stock brushes

- The stock brush library (crates/core/src/stock_brushes.rs, built by
  crates/core/src/stock_library.rs) is ProCraft's own: each brush is a variant of a familiar
  default brush type, its settings modelled on that type and every value moved by 1% to 3%, so
  each brush differs from the type it follows; distinctive names are replaced by names of our own.
  Their shapes and grains are drawn for ProCraft in code. No brush file, picture or preview from
  elsewhere is included. See docs/stock-brushes.md.
